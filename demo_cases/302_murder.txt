Victim Narrative:
The victim and the accused had a long-standing dispute. On the night of the incident, the accused attacked the victim with a knife and stabbed him multiple times in the chest and abdomen. The victim collapsed at the scene and later died in the hospital due to severe internal bleeding caused by the stab wounds.

Accused Narrative:
The accused had previously threatened the victim and had brought the knife intentionally to the location. Witnesses reported that the accused appeared angry and attacked the victim deliberately. The attack was intentional and premeditated.

Evidence Details:
CCTV footage from a nearby shop shows the accused approaching the victim with a knife. Forensic reports confirm that the victim's blood was found on the accused's clothes. Fingerprints of the accused were also found on the knife recovered from the scene.

Exception Claims:
None. The accused denies the allegations but does not claim self-defense or any legal exception.
