Victim Narrative:
The accused and the victim were involved in a physical altercation outside a bar. During the fight, the accused stabbed the victim once with a knife. The victim later died from the injury.

Accused Narrative:
The accused claims that the victim attacked him first and attempted to choke him. In fear for his life, the accused pulled out a knife and stabbed the victim to defend himself.

Evidence Details:
Medical examination showed minor injuries on the accused. There were no independent witnesses to the start of the fight. A knife was recovered at the scene with fingerprints belonging to the accused.

Exception Claims:
The accused claims self-defense and states that the stabbing occurred only to protect himself from serious harm.
