How it works

From narrative to verdict

The language model only extracts facts. Every legal judgement after that follows fixed rules, so the same case always gets the same reasoning.

01

Fact extraction

Acts, intent, harm, context, evidence and claimed defences are pulled from the narrative.

02

Element test

The likely IPC section is detected and each required element is checked against the facts.

03

Burden & evidence

Evidence is weighted and the burden of proof is assessed, including exceptions such as self-defence.

04

Panel verdict

Textualist, evidence-focused and burden-strict judges vote, and the majority decides.
