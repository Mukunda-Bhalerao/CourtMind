COURTMIND TEST CASE — EXPECTED VERDICT: NOT GUILTY

  -----------------------------------
  Victim Narrative
  -----------------------------------
  On the night of 12 March, I was
  walking near the local market when
  Rahul suddenly approached me
  aggressively. He pulled out a knife
  and attempted to stab me. I
  panicked and tried to defend
  myself. During the struggle, I
  grabbed a wooden stick that was
  lying nearby and struck Rahul once
  to stop the attack. Rahul fell to
  the ground and later died from the
  injury.

  I did not intend to kill him. I
  only acted to protect myself from
  the knife attack.
  -----------------------------------

Accused Narrative

I was walking home through the market area when Rahul confronted me and
started shouting at me. Suddenly he took out a knife and tried to attack
me. I was scared for my life and acted instinctively. I picked up a
wooden stick nearby and struck him once to stop the attack. I had no
intention of killing him and only wanted to escape the situation safely.

  -----------------------------------
  Evidence
  -----------------------------------
  1. CCTV footage from a nearby shop
  shows Rahul approaching the accused
  first. 2. A knife was recovered
  near Rahul at the scene. 3. Medical
  report shows a single blunt-force
  injury to the head. 4. A witness
  reported that Rahul attempted to
  attack the accused before the
  accused responded.

  -----------------------------------

Legal Context

The accused claims the act was done in self-defense.

Relevant principle: Right of Private Defence (IPC Sections 96–106)
states that an act done in exercise of the right of private defence is
not an offence if the force used is reasonably necessary to prevent
harm.

  ------------------------
  Expected Legal Outcome
  ------------------------

Death occurred, which may initially trigger analysis under IPC Section
299 or 300. However, the evidence indicates that the accused acted in
self-defense after being attacked with a deadly weapon.

Therefore: The right of private defence applies.

Expected CourtMind Verdict: NOT GUILTY
