Victim Narrative:
The victim invested a large sum of money with the accused after being promised high financial returns from a business venture. After receiving the money, the accused stopped responding and never returned the investment.

Accused Narrative:
The accused had convinced the victim that the investment was safe and guaranteed profits. The accused knowingly made false statements in order to persuade the victim to transfer the money.

Evidence Details:
Bank transfer records show that the victim transferred money to the accused's account. Messages between the victim and the accused contain promises of guaranteed profits. The accused withdrew the money shortly after receiving it.

Exception Claims:
None. The accused claims the business failed but denies intentionally deceiving the victim.
